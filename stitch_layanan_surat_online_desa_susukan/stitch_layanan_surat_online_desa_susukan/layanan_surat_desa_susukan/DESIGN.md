## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies an institutional yet approachable civic portal designed specifically for rural community administration in Indonesia. Its personality balances governmental authority with communal warmth—eliminating the cold, intimidating bureaucracy often associated with public office software, while preserving absolute trust, authenticity, and legal dignity. 

Drawing from **Corporate / Modern** principles infused with regional civic sensibilities, the interface uses deep botanical greens reminiscent of official rural administrative seals, softened by warm ivory foundations and ceremonial gold accents. The design caters directly to varying levels of digital literacy, from municipal operators handling high-throughput queues to elderly residents navigating via entry-level smartphones. 

Visual structures rely on clarity: generous tap targets (minimum 44px), elevated contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA standards on critical text, clean containment borders, and dual-typographic roles pairing an ultra-legible modern grotesque for interaction with a dignified transitional serif for legal paper previews.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate civic hierarchy. The chromatic structure is anchored by:
- **Primary Emerald (`#176B5B`)**: Applied to high-priority interactive touchpoints, affirmative CTAs, active steppers, and confirmed status markers.
- **Deep Slate Emerald (`#0F3D3E`)**: The structural anchor utilized for system persistence (collapsible sidebar, login canopy, and urgent floating snackbars/toasts).
- **Warm Gold Accent (`#E8B95B`)**: Reserved for active navigation items, ceremonial emphasis, and primary page actions.
- **Deep Ink Neutral (`#1E302D`)**: The primary text color, formulated with green undertones to avoid the harshness of pure black while providing 12.8:1 contrast over ivory.

The base canvas rests upon **Ivory (`#F7F8F5`)**, providing visual relief compared to clinical whites. Interactive surfaces and operational dashboards elevate out of the canvas on **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**. Status tags utilize dedicated paired tokens with minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratios:
- **Menunggu (Pending)**: Amber Soft (`#FFF3DC`) text Amber Deep (`#9A6100`)
- **Diverifikasi (Verified)**: Blue Soft (`#E3EEFB`) text Cobalt (`#245A9E`)
- **Perlu Perbaikan (Revision)**: Tangerine Soft (`#FDEBD3`) text Tangerine Deep (`#B45309`)
- **Disetujui / Selesai (Approved / Complete)**: Mint Pale (`#E1F3EC`) text Forest Pine (`#176B5B` / `#0F5B49`)
- **Ditolak (Rejected)**: Crimson Soft (`#FBE4E4`) text Crimson Deep (`#B02A2A`)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme clarity, fast scanning, and unambiguous letter differentiation. 

The primary interface font is **Inter**, configured with tailored optical adjustments across tabular grids and form labels. For numeric operational tracking (NIK, No. KK, No. Registrasi), monospace numerical glyphs (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`) must be used to preserve column alignment. 

A dedicated typographic layer is reserved for **Surat Resmi (Official Legal Letter Document Preview & DomPDF)**:
- Legal previews shift to **Georgia** (serif fallback: Times New Roman, serif) to reflect the traditional physical aesthetic of Indonesian civil village administration.
- The standard letter body uses `letter-legal-body` set at 12px with a wide 1.83 line-height (`22px`), maintaining the required formal cadence.
- Eyebrows, module tags, and table column headers utilize `label-eyebrow` set in uppercase with a `+0.15em` tracking offset for rapid data processing.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a structured **Fluid-Hybrid Dashboard Grid**:
- **Canvas Bounds**: Maximum content enclosure width is strictly clamped at `1360px` with centered alignment on ultra-wide screens.
- **Desktop Grid (>900px)**: Persistent left sidebar width fixed at `244px`, accompanied by a `64px` sticky topbar. The primary viewport utilizes a dynamic 12-column fluid grid with `14px` (`0.875rem`) to `16px` (`1rem`) gutters. Card interior padding is standardized to `18px` (`1.125rem`), and outer canvas margins to `28px` (`1.75rem`).
- **Mobile & Tablet Reflow (≤900px)**: The fixed navigation switches to an off-canvas drawer accessed via a minimum 44px hamburger icon. Column layouts reflow:
  - Metric statistics drop from 4 columns to a 2x2 grid on tablet, and single-column stacked on viewport widths below 400px.
  - Form splits and verification comparison views reflow to single stacked columns.
  - Tables wrap inside an isolated horizontal scroll track (`overflow-x: auto`) with visual fade cues.
- **Hardware Accommodations**: Safe area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-top)`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) are incorporated into base layout containers to support progressive web application (PWA) installation on edge-to-edge mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle **Ambient Shadows** and strict tonal contrast rather than heavy skeletal lines, matching an airy, contemporary civic workflow:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Ivory background (`#F7F8F5`), zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Surface pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate botanical-tinted ambient drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 28px rgba(20, 55, 47, 0.06);`
  Surfaces are framed with an imperceptible hairline boundary: `1px solid #E7ECE8` to prevent blur-bleeding on uncalibrated consumer screens.
- **Level 2 (Sticky Headers & Floating Navigation)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(20, 55, 47, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Confirmation Sheets & Drawers)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px rgba(15, 61, 62, 0.18)` positioned over a darkened backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 61, 62, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
- **Level 4 (Toasts & Ephemeral Feedback)**: Floating at the bottom center. Background anchored in deep slate emerald (`#0F3D3E`), decorated with a solid 3px left accent strip in warm gold (`#E8B95B`), and projected via `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled shape hierarchy that communicates structural reliability while softening operational friction for non-technical users:

- **Cards & Primary Surface Containers**: Clamped to a standard `16px` border-radius (`1rem`), softening dashboard data modules while retaining structural grid harmony.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Dropdowns, Action Buttons)**: Standardized to `10px` (`0.625rem`) corner rounding, balancing ergonomics with tactile clickability.
- **System Tags, Badges, and Micro Chips**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`), distinguishing status indicators and metadata markers from clickable square-corner buttons.
- **Official Letter Preview Sheet**: Sharp paper aesthetic with crisp `4px` edges when rendering virtual A4 correspondence envelopes on screen.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Forest Emerald (`#176B5B`), text `#FFFFFF`, height 42px, radius 10px, padding 0 20px, font weight 600 (Inter 14px). Hover state transitions to `#125649`. Active state scales to 0.98.
- **Secondary Action**: White container with a solid 1px border (`#E7ECE8`), text `#1E302D`. Hover shifts background to `#F7F8F5`.
- **Accent (Featured Task / Buat Pengajuan)**: Warm Gold (`#E8B95B`), text `#0F3D3E` (bold), height 42px, radius 10px.
- **Destructive (Tolak / Hapus)**: Soft Crimson base (`#FBE4E4`) with crimson text (`#B02A2A`), transitioning to solid crimson (`#D64545`) with white text on explicit confirmation dialogs.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Continuous pill shape (radius 9999px) with horizontal padding of 10px and vertical padding of 3px.
- Text sized at 11px, bold, tracking +0.02em.
- Paired strict color tokens:
  - *Menunggu*: Background `#FFF3DC`, Text `#9A6100`
  - *Diverifikasi*: Background `#E3EEFB`, Text `#245A9E`
  - *Perlu Perbaikan*: Background `#FDEBD3`, Text `#B45309`
  - *Disetujui*: Background `#E1F3EC`, Text `#176B5B`
  - *Ditolak*: Background `#FBE4E4`, Text `#B02A2A`
  - *Selesai*: Background `#E1F3EC`, Text `#0F5B49`

### Form Controls & Inputs
- Vertical stack layout: Label pinned above input, sized at 12px, weight 600, color `#1E302D`.
- Field Container: Background `#F7F8F5`, border 1px solid `#E7ECE8`, radius 10px, minimum height 44px (touch target safe).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#176B5B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(23, 107, 91, 0.15)`).
- Error state: Border `#D64545`, error caption displayed below input in 12px crimson.
- Helper instructions: 12px text in `#74817D`.

### Checkbox & Radio
- Minimum tap box of 44x44px enclosing an 18x18px control element.
- Selected state fills with `#176B5B` featuring a crisp white inner check or circular core. Radius for checkboxes is 4px; radios are 50% circular.

### Cards & Data Tables
- Cards use `#FFFFFF`, radius 16px, ambient shadow, and 18px interior padding.
- Tables feature a `#F7F8F5` header row using uppercase 10px typography (`#74817D`). Data rows are separated by 1px solid `#E7ECE8`. Row hover applies a subtle mint tint (`#E8F1ED`) across the entire interactive row.
- Identity obfuscation: Sensitive NIK 16-digit entries mask middle digits by default (`320901******0001`) with a reveal toggle for authorized operators.

### Progress Timeline (Alur Status Surat)
- 5-step responsive horizontal card track (collapsing to a stepped vertical track on mobile): *Menunggu*, *Diverifikasi*, *Persetujuan*, *Disetujui*, *Selesai*.
- Completed steps feature a `#176B5B` badge with a white checkmark. The current active step highlights with a solid emerald container and white text, while forthcoming steps remain subdued in white with `#E7ECE8` borders and muted text.

### Official Letter Paper Preview (Pratinjau Surat Resmi)
- Virtual paper card rendered with pure white background, crisp 4px corners, and subtle bordered depth simulating physical paper.
- Header block (Kop Surat) includes village logo, official serif headers, horizontal double-line separator, auto-generated registration code, and dedicated space for verification QR codes.
